What I Work On

Tube Amp Repair

Tube amps fail in ways that are easy to guess at and expensive to guess wrong about. A hum can be filter capacitors, a heater fault, a bad ground, or a tube that is only bad under load. Swapping parts until it goes away is how a cheap repair turns into a bill for parts you did not need.

Synth Repair

Vintage synths fail at the component level, and the parts that fail are often the ones nobody stocks any more. That is the part most shops will not take on - it means sourcing, testing, and calibrating rather than ordering a board and dropping it in.

Organ & Rhodes

Electromechanical keyboards are half electronics and half machine, and they need someone willing to work on both. A Leslie that has lost its speed is a mechanical problem. A Hammond that hums is an electronic one. A Rhodes that plays unevenly is neither - it is geometry.
