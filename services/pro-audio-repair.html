Pro Audio & Outboard Repair in Indianapolis

I work as a backline technician as well as a bench tech, so professional audio gear is familiar territory rather than an exception. Preamps, DIs, outboard, and touring backline all come through here.

Common Faults

What Usually Comes In

01

Dead or intermittent channels

Traced through the signal path stage by stage rather than swapping modules until it works.

02

Power supply and rail failures

A missing or sagging supply rail takes out everything downstream. On a recent Avalon U5 the positive rail was gone entirely - the cause turned out to be an open transformer winding, not the supply board everyone suspects first.

03

Transformer faults

Open windings and failed input or output transformers, which are frequently misread as a fault in the circuit they feed.

04

Noise and hum

Grounding problems, failing electrolytics, and supply ripple appearing where it should not.

05

Connectors and mechanical

Jacks, switches, and connectors - the parts that actually take the abuse on touring gear.

Note: If a piece failed at a show and you need it back fast, say so up front - rush service is available and it changes how I schedule the bench.
