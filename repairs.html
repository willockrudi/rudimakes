Not Local?

Mail-In Repair

Most of the write-ups on this page describe a specific fault, not a specific city. If you found one because your gear is doing the same thing, you do not have to be in Indiana for me to fix it.

1

Email Before You Ship

Tell me the make, model, and what it is doing. I will tell you whether it is worth shipping, roughly what to expect, and how to pack it. Do not send anything before this step.

2

Pack It Properly

Tubes and reverb tanks travel separately - they are what break in transit. Double-box with two inches of padding on every side and insure it for its real value. You pay shipping both ways.

3

Same Bench, Same Process

It goes on the same bench as everything else here. You get the diagnosis, photos where they help, and a written estimate before any repair happens. Nothing proceeds without your approval.

4

Back To You

Repaired, tested, documented, and packed the way it should have arrived. Same 30-day labor warranty as a local job.

Smaller items - pedals, modules, voice boards, synth chip sets - ship cheaply and I take those from anywhere in the world. For heavy amps from overseas, freight usually costs more than the repair; email me and I will tell you honestly whether it is worth it.
