The Fine Print

Shipping, Returns & Warranty

30-Day Functional Warranty

Every instrument is serviced and tested before listing. If something I sold you stops working within 30 days, contact me and I'll make it right - repair, replacement, or refund.

Returns

14-day returns on instruments if the item isn't as described. Buyer covers return shipping unless I got the description wrong, in which case I cover it. Items must come back in the condition they left. Parts and kits are non-returnable once installed - check the fitment list before you order, and email me if you're unsure.

Shipping

Ships from Indianapolis via UPS, insured for the full purchase price. Shipping is calculated per item based on real weight and box size. Orders ship within 2 business days and you'll get tracking by email. Free local pickup by appointment.

Used Vintage Electronics

Most of this gear is decades old. I document condition honestly, including the cosmetic flaws, and I tell you exactly what was serviced. If you want more photos or details on anything before you buy, just ask.

Sales Tax

Indiana sales tax is collected on orders shipped within Indiana.

Rudi Makes is the trading name of Filament LLC, an Indiana limited liability company. Your order is fulfilled personally by Rudi Willock in Indianapolis.

On your statement the charge appears as FILAMENT AUDIO - that's us, not a mistake. If you don't recognize a charge, email me before disputing it and I'll sort it out same day.
